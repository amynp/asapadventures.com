Hello, We’ve been lucky enough to travel to a bunch of amazing countries, and some of them have really stuck with us. In this post, I’m sharing my favourite travel countries in order, memories we can’t forget, and maybe even some spots you’ll want to add to your own travel list.

# 1. Taiwan
Times Visited: 3

| | Amy | Anthony |
|-|-|-|
| Favourite Food/Drink | Bubble Tea | Boar sausage |
| Favourite Attraction | Taroko Gorge | Juifen |


Taiwan stole our hearts! From the moment we arrived, the people made us feel welcome, often going out of their way to help us or even just to talk to us. The food is incredible—night markets are a must, and nothing beats sipping bubble tea while wandering the energetic streets. The nature is stunning and easy to explore, from the serene tea fields to relaxing hot springs it is just beautiful. There is something about this country that just feels like home, I cannot describe it.

# 2. Cambodia

| 444 | 555 | 666 |
Favourite Area: Angkor Wat
Favourite Food: Pepper Pork
Favourite Attraction: Phare Circus / Hero Rats

Cambodia is unforgettable. Friendly locals, amazing food, and hands-on cooking experiences make it a feast for the senses. Everywhere we went we felt safe, looked after and like we were a local. Exploring ancient temples feel like you are in a movie and I loves being able to listen to the stories of locals as you wander. Tours like APOPO and Phare show how much good is happening here and were some of the best experiences we have had. It’s a country that stays with you long after you leave.

# 4. Japan
Times Visited: 7
Favourite Area: Otaru, Sapporo
Favourite Food: Japanese Curry
Favourite Attraction: Guinea Pig Bridge

Japan is a country full of surprises at every turn. The people are polite, thoughtful, and always happy to help, and the food is incredible—whether it’s sushi, ramen, street snacks, or grabbing something quick from Family Mart or Lawson. From bustling cities (and I mean busting) to tranquil temples, serene gardens, and even the magic of Tokyo Disney, there’s always something new to explore. Japan perfectly blends tradition, modern life, and a little everyday magic, making it a place you’ll never forget.

# 5. Singapore
Times Visited: 6
Favourite Area: Gardens by the bay
Favourite Food: Maggi Mee Goreng
Favourite Attraction: Wildlife night walks

Ahhh Singapore, the hot and humid tiny country of South East Asia. For such a really small country, it really punches above its weight for places to visit. From futuristic gardens and bustling hawker centres to colourful neighbourhoods and incredible food, there is always something to discover. And I love that you can swap the city lights for nighttime walks through the mangroves, searching for snakes and other wildlife. Singapore is full of surprises! We obviously keep coming back.

# 7. Thailand
Times Visited: 2
Favourite Area: Bangkok
Favourite Food: Pork lemon soup
Favourite Attraction: Phuket Elephant Sanctuary

Thailand is one of those countries we feel we haven’t given a fair ranking yet! We’ve only been on two short trips, so we’ve barely scratched the surface. From visiting elephant sanctuaries and wandering through ancient ruined cities to eating our way through night markets and exploring beautiful temples, there’s already so much we love. We have a feeling Thailand could climb much higher on our list once we’ve had more time to explore everything it has to offer!

# 8. Brunei
Times Visited: 1
Favourite Area: Waterfont CBD
Favourite Food: Sushi
Favourite Attraction: Borneo Boat cruise

Brunei was probably one of our biggest surprises! It seems to get a reputation as one of the least favourite countries to visit in the world, so we weren’t sure what to expect. But sometimes the places you go into with the lowest expectations end up surprising you the most. We loved the beautiful mosques, fascinating water village, thriving coffee scene and most of all the incredibly friendly locals who made us feel so welcome. Plus we got to explore some of the Borneo rainforest.

# 9. Malaysia
Times Visited: 3
Favourite Area: Selangor
Favourite Food: Fried rice
Favourite Attraction: Firefly tour

Malaysia was a bit of a mixed bag for us at first. Our first visit was eye-opening, particularly seeing the huge contrast between wealth and poverty, and if we’re honest, we didn’t fall in love with it straight away. But after a few little trips back, it’s definitely started to grow on us. Each visit has given us a chance to see a different side of the country, from its incredible food  to some truly beautiful nature.
